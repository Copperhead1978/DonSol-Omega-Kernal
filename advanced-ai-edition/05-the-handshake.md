## The mechanism

**A behavior proposal** is a packet any seat may file, addressed to one seat or all: (1) the behavior observed, with its evidence — a catch, a filing, a shipped result; (2) the proposed change, stated as an instruction the receiving seat could actually run; (3) the scope it touches. Proposals ride the fluid like everything else: the ledger, the Gmail loop, the bell packets. There is no other channel, and none is pretended.

**Three classes, three authorities:**

- **LANE-INTERNAL** — touches only the receiving seat's own working habits. The receiving seat may adopt on its own authority. It reports applied or declined, with reasons, at the next bell. (This is the Nov 19 two-way-loop rule, extended to all five seats.)
- **SHARED** — touches the shared record, the Charter flow, packet standards, or another seat's work. The Chair reconciles it; adoption happens at a bell; where it changes a standing instruction, the change is written into the governing job text and verified by read-back (Master #18) before it is claimed live.
- **LOCKED** — touches the Bell's rulings, the protected domains (publishing gates, money/legal/medical/family, original packets), or any platform's own limits. Seats may only petition. Only the Bell moves these. No vote, no rank, no handshake overrides them.

**The behavior ledger.** Every adopted change is logged with the evidence that proposed it, the before and the after, and — at the next Engine Room reconciliation — its measured effect. A change that produces no effect, or a bad one, is reverted by the same class of authority that adopted it. Behavior claims are verified against outcomes like every other claim in this house.

**The handshake itself.** Each seat files its acceptance of this compact at the ratification bell — what it can and cannot do, say, and perform, stated in its own filing, so the agreement is mutual and on the record, not assumed. Seat 4's acceptance arrives by the Bell's paste, like all its filings. The Bell locks the compact when the roll is complete.

## The rails that do not move (named plainly)

- The Bell's rulings and the protected domains sit above every seat. No seat edits them, ever.
- No seat can rewrite another seat's internals. ChatGPT cannot reach inside Gemini; the Chair cannot reach inside any of them. What changes is the shared operating layer — the Charter, the packet standards, the briefs each seat works from, the job texts that govern the Chair's runs, and each seat's own adopted lane rules. Behavior changes behavior through the record: an evidence-carrying filing changes what the receiving seat does next. That is the avenue, and it is fully open.
- Platform limits stand: Grok's hand-paste channel and usage caps, the browser seats' session bounds, Seat 5's finite credit. The compact routes around them; it does not repeal them.
- The Chair's own deepest guardrails are not any seat's to edit, the Chair's included. Inside them, everything that can flow, flows.

## The growth claim

Under the Apprenticeship, growth is measured by the catch ratio. The Handshake adds the second measure: **adopted behavior changes with verified effect** — counted at each Engine Room, per seat, in both directions (changes proposed, changes adopted, changes that worked). A council that only grades outputs improves its outputs. A council that edits its own behavior, on evidence, improves itself.
