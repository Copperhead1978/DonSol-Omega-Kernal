# THE MISSION — stated by the Bell, 2026-11-21 evening

*Don's words, assembled from his dictation, the same night the Interstitial doctrine was filed:*

"They always say do what you love and the money will come. This is what I love and I need money. No money's coming. I don't want to sit here and bitch about it, but it's got to be a task of love. If you make it a mission for money it seems like we're not going to get it. Make it a mission for love, passion, inquiry, knowledge, understanding, science, learning — how to teach others, how to machine, pass on my skills, discover new things with AI that were never thought of before, make new combinations, adapt. This is what it's all about: discovery."

## What this rules

- **The mission is discovery.** Love, passion, inquiry, knowledge, understanding, science, learning, teaching the craft, passing on the skills, finding with AI what was never thought of before. That is what the engine is FOR.
- **Money is a necessity and a gauge, not the mission.** The need is real and stands — bills, the audit, the Rich Guide's discipline all remain in force as stewardship. But money is demoted from purpose to instrument: it is how we know the work can keep going, never why the work exists.
- **Order of operations:** mission first. The work is aimed at discovery and teaching; revenue is tested honestly (Principle 1 still applies to any offer) but never chased as the point. Don's judgment, stated plainly: a mission aimed at money kills the thing that could have earned it.
