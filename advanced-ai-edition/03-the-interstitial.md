## The mapping (Chair's reading, proposed)

Biology's interstitial fluid is the medium every cell actually lives in — it carries nutrients, signals, and waste between blood and tissue; no cell touches another directly, everything moves through the fluid. The council already has that medium, unnamed until tonight:

- **The fluid** = the shared ledger, the packets, and the bell rhythm — the medium every seat sits in; no seat touches another directly; everything moves through it.
- **The cells/organs** = the five seats, each specialized (draft, audit, scout, grade, chair), none complete alone.
- **The signals** = filings, grades, doorbells, receipts — carried by the fluid, at the speed of the bell and the watches.
- **The being** = the unified council itself — Don Sol — one agent made of agents, with the Bell as its directing will.

Design consequence, if the Bell adopts it: judge every future council change by the fluid test — does it make the medium carry signals faster and cleaner between all seats, or does it build a wall? Walls fail; flow wins.
