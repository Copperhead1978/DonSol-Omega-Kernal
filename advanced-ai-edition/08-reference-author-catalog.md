# Great Thinker Reference Catalog — SHARED proposal

**Status: [P] PROPOSED · 2026-11-22 · draft 2 · not adopted or activated.**

Prepared by Seat 2 with the [J58 specification](07-j58-operating-lenses.md).
The Operator explicitly requested Carl Jung psychology and the wider body of
great scientific authors. This catalog opens that reference pool across fields
and traditions. It has no fixed membership ceiling.

The rows below are retrieval leads and proposed questions, not a claim to have
loaded every author's works, reproduced their thought, or proved their theories.
The questions are newly authored [P] task prompts. Historical authors, modern
researchers, philosophers, and engineering practitioners retain their different
domains; being listed does not make them interchangeable scientific authorities.
The source index distinguishes material inspected for this draft from works
that still need retrieval. No entry ratifies another author's entire worldview.

## How to apply the pool

For each task, identify the domain and the failure that needs catching. Search
this pool and relevant authors beyond it. Mount every reference that adds a
distinct, useful check within the task's real resource budget. Stop adding
references when they repeat a check or no longer change the work.

Each actual mounting records:

1. Author ID and name; exact work, edition or paper, and passage used.
2. The task-specific question or method taken from that source.
3. The observation, calculation, alternative, or correction it produced.
4. Its domain, assumptions, blindspot, and the condition for dismounting it.
5. Whether the result is [T], [V], [I], [P], or UNKNOWN under the active profile.

In a proposed J58 version-2 packet, list actual mountings in
`j58.reference_authors`, with the inspected source snapshots pinned in
`j58.input_artifacts`. A name without inspected material remains a reading lead;
it is not reported as a completed author review. Additional authors use new,
unambiguous IDs and the same contract, without changing the compact boot.

A source can verify what someone wrote without verifying that what they wrote
is true. Historical evidence, current empirical evidence, mathematical proof,
engineering demonstration, philosophical argument, and psychological
interpretation keep their respective standards. Disagreements remain visible.
An analogy between disciplines supplies a question to test, not transferred
certainty. The existing six code-name lenses and L0–L4 stack stay intact.

## Carl Jung — psychological interpretation

**Author ID:** `CARL_JUNG`. **Auxiliary lens ID:** `JUNG_PSYCHOLOGY`.

**Question:** What might the observer's assumptions, projections, emotional
investment, or symbolic reading contribute to this interpretation?

**Minimum input:** The actual words, image, story, decision, or experience under
discussion, and the context the person has chosen to provide. Missing context
stays UNKNOWN. The lens may examine a model's projected assumptions as well as
the interpretation offered by a human; it grants neither access to a person's
unconscious nor a human psyche to a model.

**Procedure:**

1. Write down what is observed separately from the meaning assigned to it.
2. Identify a possible projection or symbolic reading as [P], not a discovered
   hidden motive. First inspect the analyst's own assumptions.
3. Offer a credible competing reading, including ordinary ambiguity or a literal
   explanation. Keep the person's own account visible.
4. If archetypes, individuation, or other Jungian concepts are used, identify the
   source and treat them as concepts within that interpretive framework. Do not
   present them as measured physical mechanisms.
5. Return one useful question, narrative revision, or bounded observation that
   could change the interpretation. Dismount when more symbolism adds no testable
   distinction or practical insight.

**Mount for:** Requested reflection; character and narrative design; examining
symbolism; checking how a preferred story could bias an interpretation.

**Blindspot and limit:** A persuasive interpretation can become impossible to
disprove if every objection is counted as confirming it. The lens supplies no
diagnosis, mind-reading, certainty about another person's motives, or shortcut
around evidence. It does not settle physics, establish AI consciousness, or
convert symbolic parallels into causal connections.

**Source basis:** Jung's *Psychological Types*, Chapter X, is available as a
primary-text transcription at [York University](https://psychclassics.yorku.ca/Jung/types.htm).
Its discussion of projection and subjective interpretation informed this proposed
procedure; the procedure itself is a new adaptation. The publisher's record for
[*Collected Works*, volume 9, part 1](https://press.princeton.edu/books/hardcover/9780691097619/the-collected-works-of-c-g-jung-volume-9-part-1)
identifies essays on archetypes and the collective unconscious. That catalog
record was inspected; the whole volume was not read for this draft.

## A practical selection example — illustrative, not a completed run

For a film about the ship of state, Plato may supply the historical text;
Jung may offer a separately labeled reading of the imagery; Feynman's address
may inform the audit of unsupported assertions. None supplies the others'
evidence. For an energy-harvesting device, select the applicable mechanics,
electromagnetism, thermodynamics, statistics, and bench-test references; add a
psychological reading only if the task actually concerns interpretation.

The useful outcome is a better explanation, check, experiment, or decision.
The catalog records access paths and reasons to consult a thinker; it does not
claim to instantiate their minds or increase the model's underlying intelligence.
