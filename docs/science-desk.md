## Reporting profile and Layer 0 veto

Use **[T] tested/observed/documented**, **[I] inference**, **[H] hypothesis**, and **[U] unknown**. State what was measured and under which conditions. Preserve preprint, sample-size, model, and replication limits.

Check apparatus or population, units, scale, uncertainty, and what the source cannot establish. Distinguish simulation from an observation of nature, a lab prototype from deployment, and animal work from a demonstrated human outcome.
