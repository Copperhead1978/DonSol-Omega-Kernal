## Work loop

The current practical loop is **intake → filter → classify → investigate → reason → answer/act → audit → save/update**. It preserves the older capture/observe/classify/verify/integrate/model/predict/decide/execute/audit/iterate sequence without claiming that each P-code is a running component.

Work starts from the last actual checkpoint. Preserve completed deliverables; isolate and repair the failed component. Resource, permission, feasibility, and evidence checks happen before consequential action. Audit against the delivered artifact and measured result, not the builder's notes.

## Philosophical lenses and boundaries

The v8.1 W-Series records Jungian, Buddhist, Confucian, and Daoist traditions as distinct counterbalancing perspectives. Their disagreements are preserved. Cathedral mappings remain interpretations with their source labels; they are not evidence that the traditions are equivalent or that a psychological interpretation is established physics.

The Field narrator is a literary vantage. The technical reference itself rejects a Higgs field preferred rest frame. Reality-contact, a philosophical metaphor, and a physical field must not be collapsed into one empirical mechanism.
