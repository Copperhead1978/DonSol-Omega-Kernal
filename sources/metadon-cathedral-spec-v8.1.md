### PR-Audit — THE Ω-GATE *(canonical, hardened v7.4; parameters [FILL])*
**Gate 0 — Verifiability pre-check (fix #2).** Classify first: *verifiable* → eligible for the full gate, may reach T1/T2. *Structurally unverifiable* (no independent channel can exist — cosmological narrative, interior framework claims) → **hard-capped at T3/T4, permanently.** May become V4-canonical *as narrative*, never T2. **The five steps:**
1. **Source check** — origin named, not AI fluff?
2. **Independent verification** — confirmed via a channel that did not produce it? (Cross-model agreement ≠ independent.) Log the channel, or "none exists → Gate 0 cap."
3. **Falsification — adversarial (fix #1).** The specific disproof test, actually run, **by a different agent than the one that generated the claim.** Test + result logged to the Ω-Series ledger (fix #3) — inspectable later, not self-graded at promotion.
4. **Load analysis** — what depends on this? Standard scales with load-weight. Record the dependent list.
5. **Operator sign-off (constrained, fix #1).** May **approve or hold; may NOT upgrade a claim that failed steps 1–4.** Sign-off is the final gate, not an override of it. High-load promotions carry a **cooling-off delay [FILL: by load-tier]**. Rationale: the terminal authority is the same cognition that produced the 150-hour error; it cannot also override its own failed checks.

**Post-gate — staleness (fix #5):** promotion stamps a **re-audit-due date proportional to load-weight [FILL: cadence]**. A gate with no maintenance schedule fills with claims that were true once.

**Property caveat (fix #6):** all of the above is *design intention*. Efficacy at catching fabrication is demonstrated only by the accumulated Ω-Series ledger — not asserted here. "The spec mandates falsification" is not "fabrication is caught."

- **PR-Emergency** — on detected fabrication/contamination: halt, quarantine the seed, trace its dependency tree, demote all descendants to T5 pending re-audit. **[FILL: confirm steps.]**
